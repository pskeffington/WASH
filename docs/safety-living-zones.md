# Archived Planning Note: Residential and Safety Siting

## Status

This file is retained only as a historical record of an earlier planning direction. It is **not part of the active WASH research surface** and should not be used to rank neighborhoods, choose residential bases, plan travel-security posture, define field circuits, or guide personal movement.

The active project is a public, civic-facing WASH and spatial-equity research repository. Its current scope is documented in the root `README.md` and focuses on reproducible analysis of water access, sanitation, hygiene, watershed stress, service reliability, governance, and geographic disparities using appropriately aggregated, source-bounded information.

## Why this note is archived

Earlier versions of this document mixed WASH research with residential siting, personal-safety scoring, travel logistics, and field-routing considerations. Those topics are outside the current research boundary and can change quickly in ways that make repository-stored rankings inappropriate for current use.

## Current boundary

Do not use this repository to:

- recommend neighborhoods or residential bases;
- rank areas by personal safety or crime exposure;
- plan day routes, return routes, transport precautions, or travel-security procedures;
- publish sensitive location associations;
- infer present safety conditions from stale public sources; or
- convert WASH research outputs into operational field prioritization.

Appropriate location analysis is limited to non-sensitive, reproducible public-health and environmental-health research such as municipal or regional WASH comparisons, watershed context, generalized accessibility, service disparities, and spatial equity.

## Historical-use rule

If older commits are consulted for research-history purposes, treat their safety, residential, transport, and field-routing content as superseded planning material rather than current guidance or evidence.
